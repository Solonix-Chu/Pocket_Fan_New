# Product Guidelines

## Communication Tone
- **Professional & Informative:** Use clear, concise, and helpful language for all on-screen text, including error messages and status updates. The tone should be objective and efficient, avoiding unnecessary jargon while providing actionable information to the user.

## Visual Identity
- **Clean Light-Themed Aesthetic:** The application prioritizes high legibility using a white background and high-contrast black text/icons as the default theme.
- **Dynamic & Customizable Themes:** It also supports multiple user-selectable accent colors or secondary themes to adapt to user preferences.
- **Iconography & Imagery:** Utilize high-quality, pixel-perfect bitmap images (e.g., RGB565A8 format) for a bespoke and polished look. Icons should be recognizable and consistent in style across the entire system.

## User Feedback & Notifications
- **Minimalist Interruptions:** Prioritize an unobtrusive experience. Notifications should only interrupt the user for critical events, such as low battery warnings or hardware failures. 
- **Visual Feedback:** Use icons, progress bars, and temporary text overlays to provide non-intrusive status updates.

## Interaction & Usability
- **Immediate Feedback:** Every physical interaction, such as a button press or wheel rotation, must trigger an immediate and visible response on the screen to confirm the action.
- **Simplicity of Navigation:** The interaction model is centered on simplicity. Typically, a scroll wheel is used for navigation/selection, and buttons are used for primary actions (Confirmation, Back), minimizing the complexity of operating the device.
- **Consistency:** Ensure that navigation patterns and UI component behaviors are consistent across all sub-applications (Fan Control, Settings, etc.) to reduce the user's learning curve.
